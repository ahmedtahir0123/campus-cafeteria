FOUR EASY STEPS

Your Order, Made Easy

From choosing your meal to picking it up.

01 🍽️

Choose Your Items

Check out the menu, decide what you'd like, and remember your selected items and prices.

02 💳

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your order, confirm the total, and pay using the payment methods accepted at the counter.

03 🎟️

Collect Your Token

Take your order token or receipt. Keep it safe because you may need it to identify your order.

04 🥡

Pick Up Your Food

Wait in the designated pickup area. Listen for your token number and collect your order when called.

⏰

PLAN AHEAD

A Little Tip About Peak Hours

The cafeteria may get busy during lunch breaks and between classes. Allow extra time for ordering and food preparation, especially during rush hours.

  • Decide what you want before reaching the counter.
  • Keep your payment ready.
  • Wait patiently if there is a queue.
📍

PICKUP GUIDELINES

Collect Your Order Smoothly

Help everyone get their food quickly by following the cafeteria's pickup guidelines.

  • Keep your token or receipt with you.
  • Wait in the marked pickup area.
  • Check your items before leaving.
  • Ask staff for help if your order is delayed.
