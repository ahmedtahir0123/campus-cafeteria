FOUR EASY STEPS

Your Order, Made Easy

From choosing your meal to picking it up.

01 🍽️

Choose Your Items

Check the menu, decide what you would like, and note your selected items and prices.

02 💳

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your order, confirm the total, and pay using the accepted payment methods.

03 🎟️

Collect Your Token

Collect your order token or receipt and keep it safe. You may need it to identify your order.

04 🥡

Pick Up Your Food

Wait in the pickup area and listen for your token number. Collect your order when called.

⏰

PLAN AHEAD

Busy During Peak Hours?

The cafeteria may get busy during lunch breaks and between classes. Allow extra time for ordering and food preparation.

  • Decide what you want before reaching the counter.
  • Keep your payment ready.
  • Wait patiently in the queue.
📍

PICKUP GUIDELINES

Collect Your Order Smoothly

Help everyone receive their food efficiently by following the cafeteria's pickup guidelines.

  • Keep your token or receipt with you.
  • Wait in the designated pickup area.
  • Check your items before leaving.
  • Ask staff for help if your order is delayed.
